# Disclaimer

vicine is a terminal front-end for content that is already publicly
accessible on the web. Every stream or download it serves comes from
external, non-affiliated sources — movie/series data is scraped from the
hicine API (`api.hicine.sbs`) and anime from hianime (`hianime.at`, embeds
hosted by ZokoAnime/`hls.1embed.buzz`).

- Use vicine at your own risk, in accordance with your government and
  local laws.
- The project has no control over the content it displays; the developers
  are not accountable for how you use copyrighted material obtained
  through it.
- If your site is listed or scraped by this project, the scraping methods
  are public in the source. Take whatever measures you need against them.
- DMCA or copyright complaints concern the hosting sites themselves, not
  this project — forward them to the respective provider.
- Do not harass the maintainer.

For anything else, open a GitHub issue.
